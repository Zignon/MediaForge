# Media Forge — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Media Forge is a video conversion application for Windows. It is designed to work entirely on your own computer.

## What Media Forge does with your data

- **Your video files never leave your computer.** Conversion is performed locally by the bundled FFmpeg engine. Media Forge does not upload, stream, or transmit your media to any server.
- **No account, no sign-in, no tracking.** Media Forge does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, or any personal information.
- **No network connections.** The application makes no network requests of its own. (Updates are delivered through the Microsoft Store, which is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.)
- **Metadata removal.** When the "Remove all metadata" option is enabled (the default), Media Forge strips titles, tags, chapters, and encoder information from converted files. This reduces the personal information embedded in your output files; it does not send that information anywhere.

## Information stored on your device

Media Forge saves your preferences (output folder, format choices, quality settings) in the application's local data folder so they persist between sessions. It also reads basic hardware information from Windows (CPU model, graphics adapter name) to decide which video encoders to offer. This information stays on your device and is shown only to you in the app's hardware report.

Uninstalling Media Forge removes this data.

## Children

Media Forge does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
