## Description (≤ 10,000)

Media Forge turns any video into the format you need — fast — by using the hardware already in your PC.

Drop in MKV, MPEG, AVI, MOV, WMV, TS, WebM or FLV files and convert them to MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI or MPEG-TS with H.264, H.265/HEVC, AV1, VP9 or MPEG-2. Media Forge detects your graphics card on launch and encodes on NVIDIA NVENC, Intel Quick Sync or AMD AMF when it can, falling back to every core of your CPU when it can't. No configuration needed: pick a preset, press Start forging, and let the hardware do the work.

BUILT FOR SPEED
• GPU encoding on NVIDIA, Intel and AMD graphics — verified on your machine, not just advertised
• Hardware-accelerated decoding with automatic software fallback
• Multi-core software encoding with x264, x265 and SVT-AV1 when no GPU encoder is available
• Convert several files at once

TOTAL CONTROL, OR NONE AT ALL
• One-click presets for universal MP4, small HEVC, AV1 archives, web-ready WebM and lossless remuxing
• Constant-quality or target-bitrate modes, speed/quality balance, resolution and frame-rate scaling
• AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, MP3, Opus or FLAC audio — or keep the original track untouched
• Keep subtitles where the container supports them
• Remove all metadata (titles, tags, chapters, encoder stamps) for clean, private output files

MADE FOR BATCHES
• Drag-and-drop files or whole folders
• Live progress with frames per second, speed, output size and time remaining
• Save to a folder of your choice or right next to each source file
• "Convert with Media Forge" from the right-click menu on any video

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your computer. Media Forge never uploads your videos, never phones home, and collects no data.

Media Forge includes FFmpeg (GPL). Source code and license details are available from the app's homepage.
